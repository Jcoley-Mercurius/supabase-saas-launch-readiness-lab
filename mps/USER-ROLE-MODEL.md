# Supabase SaaS Launch-Readiness Lab — User & Role Model

Status: Approved  
MPS version: v1.0

| ID | Actor | Context and job | Intended product authority | Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MPS-ACT-001 | SaaS founder or product owner | Preparing to launch or onboard paying users | Browse proof, compare states, read the report, submit an inquiry | Public synthetic evidence only |
| MPS-ACT-002 | Agency or development team | Responsible for client delivery or remediation | Same public actions; client-system authority is not assumed | Public synthetic evidence only |
| MPS-ACT-003 | Josh / auditor-developer | Operates the lab and qualifies work | Maintain synthetic demonstrations, review inquiries, confirm fit, establish separate authorization/scope | Lab operations and necessary inquiry data |
| MPS-ACT-004 | Upwork prospect or portfolio reviewer | Judges relevance and professional capability | Browse without an account or private access | Public synthetic evidence only |

## Role boundaries

- R1 creates no customer accounts, client workspaces, or in-product auditor roles.
- Public visitors cannot upload an application, credentials, evidence, or secrets.
- An agency inquiry does not prove authority over a client system.
- MTS implements authentication, authorization, tenancy, and operator access controls.
