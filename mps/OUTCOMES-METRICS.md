# Supabase SaaS Launch-Readiness Lab — Outcomes & Metrics

Status: Approved measurement definitions; numeric baselines and targets pending launch  
MPS version: v1.0

| Outcome | Metric | Definition | Initial interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| MPS-OUT-001 — Buyers understand launch risk | MPS-MET-001 — Proof comprehension | Evidence that qualified visitors identify the covered risk areas and authorized-service boundary | Do not infer understanding from page views alone |
| MPS-OUT-002 — Buyers can verify remediation proof | MPS-MET-002 — Scenario completion | Visitor reaches a before/after evidence result for at least one scenario | Shows engagement with proof, not purchase intent |
| MPS-OUT-003 — Josh wins differentiated work | MPS-MET-003 — Qualified inquiry conversion | Qualified authorized-engagement inquiries attributable to the lab divided by unique visitors | Establish a baseline before setting a numeric target |
| MPS-OUT-003 — Proof supports selling | MPS-MET-004 — Proposal proof reuse | Relevant Upwork proposals using the lab as evidence | Track directionally alongside proposal quality and buyer fit |
| MPS-OUT-004 — Market learning improves the service | MPS-MET-005 — Buyer signal capture | Qualified inquiry themes, scenario interest, objections, and requested scope captured without sensitive data | Use for R2 prioritization; traffic alone is not demand |

## Guardrails

- Do not collect secrets, production credentials, source code, or customer records for measurement.
- Do not use duplicate inquiries to inflate demand.
- Do not label an inquiry qualified until role, need, authorization posture, and relevance are manually reviewed.
- Do not publish a numeric conversion promise until a baseline, sample size, and attribution method exist.
