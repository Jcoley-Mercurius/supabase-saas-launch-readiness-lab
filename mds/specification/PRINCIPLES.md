# Launch-Readiness Lab — MDS Principles

Status: Approved  
Version: MDS v1.0

## Evidence is the hero

Technical proof receives the strongest compositional weight. Marketing language introduces the buyer problem and route forward, but never competes with the evidence panel, finding, limitation, or report.

Implementation test: a buyer can identify what was tested, what happened, and what remains unproven before encountering a promotional claim.

## Make risk legible without oversimplifying it

Pair plain-language consequence with technical evidence. Preserve affected boundary, severity, reproduction, remediation direction, and limitations. Do not collapse nuanced states into a binary success/failure badge.

Implementation test: both a founder and an engineer can interpret the finding without either audience receiving misleading information.

## Trust comes from explicit state and limitation

Every material evidence state has an icon, label, semantic color, and explanation. Untested, unavailable, and not applicable never resemble remediated. Limitations sit beside the evidence they qualify.

Implementation test: disabling color and animation does not erase evidence meaning, and no missing result looks like a pass.

## Keep the buyer path clear beside technical depth

Scenario, report, and authorized-review routes remain consistently available without obscuring the proof. The CTA requests a conversation; it never implies authorization, acceptance, price, response time, or outcome.

Implementation test: a report-first or single-scenario visitor can reach the inquiry boundary without creating an account or completing every scenario.

## Operational rule

When principles conflict, product safety and truthful evidence outrank visual drama and conversion optimization. Report a gap instead of inventing a convention.
