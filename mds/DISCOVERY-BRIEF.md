# Supabase SaaS Launch-Readiness Lab — MDS Discovery Brief

Status: Gate 1 complete and approved; Gate 2 foundations in progress  
MDS version: v0.1-draft  
Consumed MPS: v1.0

## Experience problem

The interface must earn trust with two audiences at once: a founder who needs plain-language clarity and an engineer or agency reviewer who needs enough technical evidence to believe the work. It must also convert interest into an authorized service conversation without making the lab feel like a thin marketing funnel.

## Experience hierarchy

1. Understand what the lab proves and what it does not claim.
2. Enter a coherent synthetic SaaS context.
3. Explore one or more launch-risk scenarios.
4. Distinguish vulnerable, remediated, untested, and unavailable evidence.
5. Understand severity, consequence, reproduction, remediation, and limitation.
6. Review the full report/case study.
7. Request an authorized review or remediation conversation.

## Approved design posture

An evidence-led technical studio: credible, calm, precise, and commercially clear. The design should feel more like a polished engineering review room than a generic SaaS dashboard. It should avoid neon cyber-security clichés, excessive terminal styling, fear-first imagery, and decorative charts that do not carry evidence.

## Approved composition model

- A concise public narrative shell explains the service and provides guided entry.
- A scenario index introduces the synthetic SaaS and its four evidence pillars.
- A guided lab shell keeps scenario context, evidence state, plain-language impact, and navigation visible.
- Each scenario supports vulnerable/remediated comparison plus an explicit limitation.
- A report shell consolidates findings and keeps the authorized-engagement CTA accessible.
- The inquiry experience is short, transparent, and clearly separate from engagement acceptance.

## Required design states

- Vulnerable/failing
- Remediated/passing for the documented scenario
- Untested
- Not applicable
- Loading/running
- Unavailable
- Retry/recovery
- Inquiry unconfirmed
- Inquiry acknowledged
- Duplicate submission
- Unauthorized live-system request boundary

State communication must use label, icon/shape, and explanatory text—not color alone.

## Approved principles

- Evidence is the hero.
- Make risk legible without oversimplifying it.
- Trust comes from explicit state and limitation.
- Keep the buyer path clear beside technical depth.

## Approved identity relationship

The Supabase SaaS Launch-Readiness Lab is a standalone product identity with a discreet “Built by Josh Coley / Mercurius” endorsement. The endorsement establishes authorship and commercial trust without turning the interface into a generic Mercurius marketing page.

Gate 2 now defines exact color, typography, spacing, shape/elevation, iconography, and remaining visual-language rules before visual reference generation.
