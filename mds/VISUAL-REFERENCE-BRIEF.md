## Generation invariants

- Evidence-led technical studio; calm, rigorous, credible, and commercially clear.
- Standalone Launch-Readiness Lab identity with discreet “Built by Josh Coley · Mercurius” endorsement.
- Light-first buyer surfaces, deep-ink framing, and dark evidence panels.
- Exact approved palette: ink `#0B1220`, primary `#087A5B`, accent `#19A974`, canvas `#F4F7F9`, white surfaces, evidence `#111827`, danger `#B42318`, warning `#9A5B00`, information `#175CD3`, untested `#667085`.
- Geist-style restrained sans typography and Geist-style monospace evidence text.
- 6/10/14 px radius logic, border-first cards, restrained shadows, outline icons.
- No hacker clichés, neon cyber styling, shield/lock/bug motifs, fake terminal wallpaper, stock photography, decorative charts, or glassmorphism.
- Status never depends on color alone.
- Claims stay scoped: use “Remediated — documented test blocked,” never “secure,” “certified,” or unqualified “passed.”
- Generated text is reference content; canonical MDS copy and state remain authoritative if letterforms drift.
